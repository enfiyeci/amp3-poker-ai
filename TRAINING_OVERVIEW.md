# Training Overview

## Two Training Approaches

**Supervised Learning (Preflop, Flop, Turn, River, OSM)**
- Learned from expert poker strategies and game data
- Fast, efficient training process
- Follows proven Game Theory Optimal (GTO) principles
- Specialized for specific game situations

**Reinforcement Learning (AMP3)**
- Learned by playing against itself
- Self-discovered strategies through trial and error
- Adapts based on experience
- Unified approach across all game situations

## Key Differences

**What They Learn**
- Supervised: "What experts do" → static, proven strategies
- AMP3: "What works" → dynamic, adaptive strategies

**How They Play**
- Supervised: Same strategy against all opponents
- AMP3: Adapts to different playing styles

**Training Status**
- All models completed training
- Supervised models: Validated quality metrics
- AMP3: Full training cycle complete (120k episodes)
