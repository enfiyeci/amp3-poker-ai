# Model Descriptions (One Sentence Each)

**Preflop** - Supervised learning model achieving 79.2% validation accuracy on pre-flop decisions through expert GTO strategy imitation.

**AMP3** - Unified reinforcement learning agent with integrated opponent modeling trained through 120k episodes of self-play for full-game adaptive poker strategy.

**Flop** - Specialized supervised model for post-flop decision making achieving 68.2% training accuracy on 50,000 labeled hands.

**River** - Dedicated river street model trained on 50,000 hands with 67.1% training accuracy for final betting round decisions.

**Turn** - Turn street specialist achieving 66.8% training accuracy, designed to handle complex mid-game decision points with high strategic diversity.
