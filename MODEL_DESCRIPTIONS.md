# Model Descriptions

## Brief Model Summaries

**Preflop** - Highest accuracy, expert-validated GTO preflop decisions

**AMP3** - Unified reinforcement learning with integrated opponent modeling

**Turn** - Specialized turn street decisions with strong diversity

**River** - Dedicated river play with balanced strategic variety

**Flop** - Flop-specific decision making with moderate performance

---

**Note:** OSM (Opponent State Model) is integrated within AMP3 as its opponent modeling component, not a separate standalone model.
